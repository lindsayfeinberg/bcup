## App Description
`Bcup` is a social app for private, invite-only drinking-game communities built for adults 21 and older.

Log game outcomes with the required photo evidence, see community activity, and track competitive progress with odds and brackets.

During onboarding, users confirm they are 21+ before accessing the app.
