## Age eligibility / adult-only
- Users must self-confirm 21+ during onboarding before accessing the app.
- The app is intended only for users aged 21 and older.

## Alcohol content (draft guidance)
- The app is designed for adults 21+ to log drinking-game outcomes within private, invite-only communities.
- Content involving alcohol is presented as part of drinking-game logging and social competition (with required photo evidence).
- The app does not sell alcohol.
- The app is not intended to encourage or promote alcohol consumption to underage users.

## User-generated content (UGC)
- Users can create and share content inside private communities, including photos associated with logged games.
- Access to community content is restricted to members of the relevant private community.
