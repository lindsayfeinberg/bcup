# Alcohol Policy Wording (App Review / Age Rating Notes) - Draft

## 1) Eligibility / age gating statement (21+ only)
`Bcup` is only available to users aged 21 and older. During onboarding, users must self-confirm that they are 21+ before they can access the app.

## 2) Nature of content statement (how alcohol is represented)
`Bcup` is a private, invite-only social app for adults 21+ that supports logging of drinking-game outcomes with required photo evidence. The app does not sell alcohol and does not target underage users.

## Consistency notes (use when answering reviewer questions)
- Access to the app is gated by the in-app 21+ onboarding flow.
- Users create and share drinking-game log content within their private communities.
- Alcohol appears only as part of drinking-game logging context; the app is focused on social game logging, odds tracking, and brackets.
