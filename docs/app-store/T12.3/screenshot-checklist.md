# Screenshot Checklist (what each screenshot must communicate)

Use screenshots that match your actual app UI in V1.

## Required coverage (recommended 5-7 screenshots)
1. App icon / splash (optional if your review requires fewer)
2. Onboarding: the 21+ age confirmation screen
   - Show the “Age Confirmation” header and the button: “I am 21 or older”
3. Communities: private community / invite-only join (list or create/join view)
4. Logging: “Log New Game” flow or new-log form showing
   - community selection
   - participants selection
   - required photo evidence cue
5. Logging results: the logged game shown in feed (with photo evidence visible)
6. Odds/Stats surface (profile view or community odds indicator)
7. Brackets: bracket view showing seeded matches and progression

## Consistency checks before submission
- Any alcohol-related visuals should be framed as “drinking-game outcomes” inside the private community context.
- Do not imply purchase or sale of alcohol.
- Ensure copy on screenshots aligns with in-app gating (21+ for access).
